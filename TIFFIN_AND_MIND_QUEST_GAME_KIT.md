# 1. GAME OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE

The Dual-Quest Game System translates Youth4Impact’s core classroom outcomes into high-energy, card-driven experiential workshops. Students play in **Squads of 4–6**. Every 5–7 minutes, the game shifts to a new interactive mechanic (sorting, relay, acting, or strategy builder).

# 2. THREE-TIER TARGET GROUP BREAKDOWN

To keep students engaged at their cognitive and developmental level, cards and prompts are split into three differentiated groups:

| Parameter | 🟡 Group 1: Junior Detectives (Grades 3–4 \| Ages 8–9) | 🔵 Group 2: Investigator Squad (Grades 5–6 \| Ages 10–11) | 🟣 Group 3: Master Strategists (Grades 7–8 \| Ages 12–14) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cognitive Style** | Concrete, sensory, visual, movement-heavy. | Social, peer-oriented, cause-and-effect reasoning. | Critical analysis, nuanced dilemmas, real-world context. |
| **Day 1 Focus (Nutrition)** | Identifying food items, matching to 4 colors (Staple, Protein, Veg/Fruit, Water). No shaming. | Understanding nutrient roles (repair, focus, stamina), budget constraints, balanced proportions. | Deconstructing food marketing, hidden sugars/salts, real-world tiffin hacks, lifestyle impact. |
| **Day 2 Focus (Mental Health)** | Naming primary emotions, physical gestures, simple self-soothing (breathing, water). | Emotional intensity scale (1–5), friendship dynamics, empathy, multi-step coping. | Complex mixed emotions (jealousy, pressure, shame), assertiveness vs aggression, support mapping. |
| **Facilitator Pacing** | Fast rounds (2–3 mins per action), energetic call-and-response. | Team collaboration, timed 30-sec squad huddles. | Peer-led presentations, structured debates, strategic trade-offs. |

# 3. DAY 1 GAME: THE GREAT TIFFIN DETECTIVE
**Theme:** “What’s in My Tiffin? — Uncover the Secret Nutrients”  
**Squad Setup:** 4–6 students per team. Each squad gets a **Tiffin Tray Mat** (divided into 4 zones: Staple, Growth/Protein, Shield/Vitamins, Hydration) and starting fuel tokens.

<a id="day-1-mechanics"></a>
### 🕹️ Game Mechanics & Rules
1. **Round 1 (Tiffin X-Ray):** Squads receive food cards face down. On Game Master's whistle, they flip them and identify what nutrient superpower is missing in a sample tiffin within 45 seconds.
2. **Round 2 (Build-a-Tiffin Challenge):** Squads are dealt 8 randomized food cards. They must assemble the most balanced ₹0-extra-budget meal on their tray mat within 3 minutes and pitch it in 30 seconds.
3. **Round 3 (Munchy’s Speed Sort):** Facilitator holds up cards. Squad runners run to the **Everyday (Green Zone)** or **Sometimes (Yellow Zone)** banner.
4. **Round 4 (Hygiene & Label Relay):** Teams identify food safety hazards or package label clues to earn bonus Shield Badges.

# 4. DAY 2 GAME: THE MIND QUEST (PAUSE • POWER • PROTECT)
**Theme:** “Feelings Matter Too — Understand • Pause • Choose”  
**Squad Setup:** Same Squads of 4–6. Each squad receives a **Feelings Thermometer Slider** (Numbered 1 to 5) and a **Coping Toolbox Board**.

<a id="day-2-mechanics"></a>
### 🕹️ Game Mechanics & Rules
1. **Round 1 (Emotion Charades & Clue Spotting):** Squad actor draws an Emotion Card and acts it out silently using body posture, facial signals, and gestures. Squad guesses the emotion and identifies the body clue.
2. **Round 2 (Feelings Thermometer Calibration):** Facilitator reads a real-world student scenario. Squads calibrate the temperature dial from **1 (Mild breeze)** to **5 (Volcanic storm)** and justify why people experience different temperatures.
3. **Round 3 (Pause–Notice–Choose Challenge):** Squads receive high-stress dilemma cards. They have 60 seconds to map out:
   - 🛑 **PAUSE:** What physical grounding trick stops an impulsive reaction?
   - 👀 **NOTICE:** What emotion and physical sensation is happening?
   - 🤔 **CHOOSE:** What are 2 safe, empowering action paths?
4. **Round 4 (Coping Toolbox Relay):** Teams race to equip characters with healthy coping tools and identify their Circle of Support.

## 🗂️ DAY 2 • FLASHCARD DECK 3: PAUSE–NOTICE–CHOOSE DILEMMA CARDS

### 🟡 Group 1 (Grades 3–4) — Dilemma Cards

#### Card PNC-01: The Ball Snatcher ⚽
* **Front:** "During recess, another kid grabs your football and kicks it across the field laughing."
* **Back:**
  * 🛑 **PAUSE (5 seconds):** Freeze your feet like a statue. Take one big elephant breath (trunk up, breathe out trunk down).
  * 👀 **NOTICE:** "I feel super angry and my chest feels hot."
  * 🤔 **CHOOSE (Squad pick):**
    - ❌ *Unsafe:* Charge and push them down into the mud.
    - ✅ *Safe Path A:* Walk over firmly: "That wasn't cool, give our ball back so we can all play."
    - ✅ *Safe Path B:* Call the sports teacher or play supervisor if they refuse to return it.

#### Card PNC-02: The Spilled Paint 🎨
* **Front:** "You accidentally knock over your teammate's water cup, smudging their drawing."
* **Back:**
  * 🛑 **PAUSE:** Don't run away or blame someone else.
  * 👀 **NOTICE:** "I feel scared they will shout at me, and guilty."
  * 🤔 **CHOOSE:**
    - ❌ *Unsafe:* Say "It wasn't my fault, you put it too close!"
    - ✅ *Safe Path A:* Say immediately: "I'm so sorry, it was an accident. Let me help you clean it up and dry it."

### 🔵 Group 2 (Grades 5–6) — Dilemma Cards

* **Front:** "One student in your 4-person team is playing on their phone and refusing to do any writing. The deadline is in 20 minutes."

#### Card PNC-05: The Peer Pressure Dare 📱

#### Card PNC-06: The Burnout Spiral 🕯️

  * 🛑 **PAUSE:** Close the books. Put your feet flat on the floor. 4-7-8 breathing.
  * 👀 **NOTICE:** "I am reaching mental exhaustion. My brain cannot absorb more right now."
  * 🤔 **CHOOSE:**
    - ❌ *Unsafe:* Stay awake until 3 AM with energy drinks crying in silence.
    - ✅ *Safe Path A:* Prioritize sleep: Sleep 6 hours; wake up 30 minutes early to review key formulas with a rested mind.
    - ✅ *Safe Path B (Communicate):* Speak honestly with parents/teacher: "I studied, but I am overwhelmed tonight. I will do my best with what I know."
